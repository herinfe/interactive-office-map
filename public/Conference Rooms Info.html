Conference Rooms

Conference rooms are intended for larger team meetings, brainstorming sessions, and calls. Reserve in advance through Outlook or via the tablet just outside the door.

📅
Book through Outlook by adding the room as a resource in your meeting invite, or use the tablet outside the door.
⏱️
Please vacate promptly when your meeting ends — lingering reduces availability for others.
🗺️
Click any highlighted room on the map to see its calendar, capacity, equipment, and to book directly from this tool.
